About the Project

Environmental perception & sensor data collection.

What is PACE?

The PACE project develops innovative research methodologies to understand and measure how individuals experience comfort in urban environments, enabling sustainable data-driven urban planning decisions and smart routing applications.

Sensor data — including atmospheric conditions, air quality, noise levels, particulate matter, motion, and GNSS positioning — is collected continuously throughout the campaign and linked to each survey response, enabling rich analysis of how physical environmental factors relate to perceived comfort.

How It Works
  1. Walk — The group walks along a route while sensors record data continuously.
  2. Stop — At each designated stop, the admin unlocks the survey for participants.
  3. Survey — Participants rate environmental features, personal perception, and overall comfort on a 1–7 scale.
  4. Continue — Walk to the next stop and repeat until the campaign is complete.
