About PACE

Personalized Assessment of Urban Comfort for Explainable Routing.

What is PACE?

PACE bridges the sensory gap between objectively measured environmental and visual conditions and subjectively perceived urban comfort across seven domains: Temperature, Noise, Air Quality, Aesthetics, Urban Design, Diversity, and Accessibility.

By pairing mobile multi-modal field measurements with in-situ participant ratings, PACE supports human-centered urban planning and explainable pedestrian routing.

How a Field Walk Works
  1. Walk — The group walks along an urban route while SENSE and HALO record synchronized environmental, optical, and thermal streams.
  2. Stop — At each designated stop, the operator unlocks the in-situ survey for participants.
  3. Survey — Participants rate environmental features, personal perception, and overall comfort on a 1–7 Likert scale.
  4. Analyze — Sensor snapshots, live camera frames, and survey responses are combined on SENSE for urban comfort modeling.
