About PACE

Personalized Assessment of Urban Comfort for Explainable Routing — SENSE & HALO.

What is PACE?

PACE investigates how people perceive comfort in urban environments and bridges the gap between objectively measured environmental conditions and subjective human perception.

By combining mobile environmental sensing (SENSE), head-mounted stereo-optical and thermal capture (HALO), and in-situ participant ratings, PACE supports human-centered urban planning and explainable pedestrian routing.

How It Works
  1. Walk — SENSE and HALO record synchronized environmental, optical, and thermal data along the route.
  2. Stop — At each stop, the operator unlocks the in-situ survey.
  3. Survey — Participants rate environmental features, personal perception, and overall comfort (1–7 Likert).
  4. Analyze — Sensor streams, live frames, and survey responses are combined on SENSE for comfort modeling.
