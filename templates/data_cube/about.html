About PACE

Personalized Assessment of Urban Comfort for Explainable Routing — SENSE & HALO Platform.

What is PACE?

The PACE project investigates how people perceive comfort in urban environments and bridges the gap between objectively measured environmental conditions and subjective human perception.

By combining mobile environmental sensing (SENSE), head-mounted stereo-optical and thermal capture (HALO), and in-situ participant ratings, PACE builds the foundation for human-centered urban planning and explainable pedestrian routing.

How It Works
  1. Walk — The group walks along an urban route while SENSE and HALO record synchronized environmental, optical, and thermal data.
  2. Stop — At each designated stop, the operator unlocks the in-situ survey for participants.
  3. Survey — Participants rate environmental features, personal perception, and overall comfort on a 1–7 Likert scale.
  4. Analyze — Synchronized sensor streams, live frames, and survey responses are stored on SENSE for urban comfort modeling.
