View sensor readings and surveys on the SENSE Controller

Exports the route as a single CSV with continuous points (one every ~5 m) and matching sensor measurements — survey stops are collapsed into a single representative point. The survey JSON relinks every participant at a stop to the same GNSS id. Weather and amenities exports require an internet connection and cover the same locations/timespan as the measured data. Optionally pick a date range to limit the exported records.
